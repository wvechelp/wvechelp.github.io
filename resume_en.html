About me

As an environmental data scientist with a background in freshwater ecology and an affinity for data analysis, I am driven by the desire to unravel the information hidden within data to identify synergies between nature and society. I have experience in field-based monitoring and data-driven modelling, supplemented with a solid basis in data analysis and visualisation. Currently, I am looking for a new challenge that aligns with my interests to translate data into useful insights and actions with an added value for society.
